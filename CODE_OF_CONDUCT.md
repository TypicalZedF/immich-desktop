# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Immich Desktop to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be kind and respectful. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Focus on what is best for the project and its users. Assume good faith, and keep discussion on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, sexualized language or imagery, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge inappropriate. To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's Security tab (Report a vulnerability) and mark it as a conduct report, or contact the maintainer @TypicalZedF through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
